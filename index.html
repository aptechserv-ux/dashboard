📊 Operational Cohort Peer-Group Benchmark Methodology

To prevent distortion from comparing vastly different operational environments, statewide 1-30 rankings have been completely retired in favor of four focused, peer-based cohorts:

  • Group 1 (Commissionerates & Heavy Workload): Evaluates large urban jurisdictions and heavy litigation caseloads (>8,000 FIRs or >1,500 police strength).
  • Group 2 (Large Districts): Benchmarks extensive rural/plains agrarian geographies with high population bases (>20 Lakhs or 6,000-8,000 FIRs).
  • Group 3 (Mid-Sized Districts): Benchmarks standard district policing profiles and bifurcated administrative workflows (3,000-6,000 FIRs).
  • Group 4 (Tribal / GRPs): Evaluates specialized jurisdictions (Railway Police and Agency hill tracts) on their specific operational mandates without penalizing structural or cellular gaps.
  • Revised Parameter Set: CriMAC, Drones and Dial 112 have been removed from the rankings. APOLIS Utilization (Downloads 30% + APRs 30% + ACR 40%) is raised from 5% to 10%. Two parameters are added: eDAR (10%) — share of form 1 reports completed in eDAR Application out of road-accident FIRs ; and APOLIS File Processing (5%) — an equal 50/50 blend of the share of APOLIS files disposed by staff and the speed of disposal (average days per file, fastest unit scores 100 and slowest 0). Total weight remains 100%.
